About Me

Quality engineering through automation.

I'm a QA Automation Engineer with over 4 years of experience working across retail, financial services, and fleet management. My experience covers manual and automation testing across web, mobile, backend, and API applications.

I work primarily with Java, Selenium WebDriver, Appium, TestNG, Playwright, and TypeScript. My recent work includes dedicated Playwright frameworks for UI and API testing, automated regression suites, SQL validation, and CI/CD pipelines that help teams deliver reliable software.

Beyond writing automated tests, I enjoy designing automation frameworks that are maintainable, reusable, and easy for teams to work with. I've built automation frameworks using both Selenium with Java and Playwright with TypeScript, with a focus on clean architecture, reusable components, reporting, logging, test data, and reliable execution.

I enjoy working in Agile teams and collaborating with developers, business analysts, product owners, and fellow QA engineers to deliver quality solutions that meet business and user needs.
